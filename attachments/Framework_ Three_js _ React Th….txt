Post-Processing Pipeline

 LUT / Color Grading: Cinematic contrast LUT boosting deep blacks and warm gold midtones (⁠contrast: 1.15⁠, ⁠saturation: 1.05⁠).
 Vignette: Darkness ⁠0.6⁠, offset ⁠0.25⁠ to maintain the spotlight/theater illusion.
